# Product Overview

**ArchDraftUniversal** is an architecture drafting and diagramming tool designed to help users create, visualize, and manage architectural designs and technical diagrams.

## Core Purpose

- Provide a universal platform for creating architecture diagrams and technical drafts
- Support multiple diagram types and architectural notations
- Enable collaboration and sharing of architectural designs

## Key Goals

- Intuitive drafting experience for architects and developers
- Universal compatibility across diagram formats and export targets
- Clean, maintainable codebase that scales with feature growth
